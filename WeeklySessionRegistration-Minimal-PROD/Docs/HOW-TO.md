# How-To Guide (End Users)

A quick guide to registering for sessions with the Weekly Session
Registration app.

---

## Opening the app

The welcome page shows the current **featured event**. Select
**Get Started** (or the event card's **Register Now / View Details**) to
open the event page. If someone shared an event link with you, it opens
the right event page directly.

## The event page

- **Left**: the event's details (dates, location, type, coordinator,
  meeting link for virtual events).
- **Calendar**: each day shows how many sessions it has, color-coded —
  the legend above the calendar explains the colors (including green for
  sessions **you** are registered in and blue for ones you registered
  **for others**). Click a day to see its sessions.
- **Session list**: every session with its time and remaining seats.
  Sessions you already hold show a **Registered ✓** badge.

## Register for a session

1. Pick a session and select **Register**.
2. **Step 1 — who is it for?**
   - **For myself** (default), or
   - **For someone else** (only when the event allows it): type a name
     in the search box and pick the person from the suggestions. You'll
     see their photo, name, and email before continuing.
3. **Step 2 — details:** name and email are filled in automatically.
   Complete the required fields:
   - **Phone number** (10 digits)
   - **Department** and **Site location**
   - **Question 1** and **Question 2** (Yes/No)
4. Select **Confirm**. You'll see an on-screen confirmation, and the
   registrant receives a **confirmation email** and a **Teams
   notification** with the session details and reference number.

> **Cutoff rule:** you can't register for a session that has already
> started or starts within the event's cutoff window (typically 48
> hours). Each event may also limit how many of its sessions one person
> can hold.

## Switch to a different session

Already registered but the time no longer works?

1. On your registration card (in **My Registrations**), select
   **Switch**, or simply pick another open session on the same event.
2. Confirm. Your old seat is released, the new one is booked, and you
   get an updated confirmation email. Switching is allowed even inside
   the cutoff window.

## Cancel a registration

1. Find the card under **My Registrations** and select **Cancel**.
2. Confirm in the dialog. The seat is released and a cancellation email
   is sent.

## My Registrations

The **My Registrations** section lists your bookings with three tabs:

- **All** — everything below combined
- **Mine** — sessions you attend yourself
- **For others** — registrations you made on someone else's behalf
  (you can switch or cancel these too; the card shows who it's for)

The tab you pick also filters the calendar's highlight colors.

## Add a session to your calendar

On any of **your own** registration cards, select **Add to Calendar** —
an Outlook calendar event is created for you automatically (with the
meeting link for virtual sessions).

## Share an event

Select **Share Event**, pick people from the search suggestions, add an
optional note, and send. They receive an email with a link that opens the
app directly on that event's page.

## Need help?

Contact the event's **coordinator or POC** (shown in the event details
panel), or your app administrator.
